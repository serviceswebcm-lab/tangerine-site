Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Tangerine Cat Radio Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Tangerine Cat Radio YouTube channel to upload and manage the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

With the channel owner's authorization, the tool accesses the owner's YouTube account to upload videos, set thumbnails, and manage playlists. It does not access, collect or store data from any other user.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private infrastructure and are used only to publish the channel's own content. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Authorization can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Contact

For questions, contact the channel through YouTube.
